Our Story

Raymond Store - Krishna Textiles {Fabrics & Tailoring}

Raymond Store – Krishna Textiles is Jaipur's premier destination for fine fabrics and bespoke tailoring, proudly representing the iconic Raymond brand. Located in the heart of Mansarovar Sector 3, we bring decades of Raymond's textile heritage to your doorstep, offering an unmatched collection of suiting, shirting, and home fabrics crafted for every occasion — from boardroom meetings to festive celebrations.

With a stellar rating of 4.9 out of 5, our store has earned the trust and admiration of customers across Jaipur through exceptional craftsmanship, personalized service, and honest pricing. Our expert tailors ensure every garment fits like it was made for you — because it was. Whether you're looking for the perfect coat-pant set, a sharply stitched suit, or vibrant fabric combinations, Krishna Textiles is your one-stop solution for style and substance.
